# Stewards of Calradia

> Early Development

Stewards of Calradia is a long-term Bannerlord expansion focused on strengthening the institutions that hold Calradia together.

## Vision

Stewards of Calradia aims to make Calradia feel like a living, governed world. Rather than focusing on making the player more powerful, the project focuses on communication, administration, record keeping, and continuity.

## Planned Systems

- Heralds
- Couriers
- Institutions
- Registries
- Sunmark riding academy
- Stewardship

## Design Pillars

### Institutions over isolated mechanics

Systems should make Calradia feel like a functioning civilization: heralds, couriers, registries, guilds, stewardship offices, and academies.

### The world should communicate

Important events should naturally reach the player through believable channels such as announcements, invitations, proclamations, rumors, and notices.

### History should matter

The world should remember ownership, lineage, achievements, offices, records, and institutional continuity.

### Relationships need infrastructure

Communication between nobles, clans, kingdoms, and institutions should be supported by letters, messengers, invitations, and meeting requests.

### Static data can become living systems

Where appropriate, static records and items should become persistent systems: horse registries, breeding, training, markets, and institutional records.

## Current Status

The project is currently in Phase 0: foundations. The immediate goal is to establish module loading, DLL loading, a first `MBSubModuleBase`, and a reliable debugging workflow.
